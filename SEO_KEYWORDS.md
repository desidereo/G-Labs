## Checklist for new/updated pages
- [ ] `<title>` — under 60 chars, includes main keyword + "| G-Labs"
- [ ] `<meta name="description">` — 150–160 chars, compelling, includes keyword
- [ ] `<meta name="keywords">` — 5–10 terms, comma-separated
- [ ] `<link rel="canonical">` — full URL (https://www.g-labs.software/...)
- [ ] Open Graph: og:title, og:description, og:url, og:image, og:type
- [ ] Twitter: twitter:card, twitter:title, twitter:description, twitter:image (optional)
- [ ] `robots`: index, follow (or noindex for payment/checkout)
- [ ] Schema.org (optional): WebPage, Product, or SoftwareApplication as appropriate
