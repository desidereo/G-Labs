# Global Intelligence Dashboard: Business Case & User Guide
## TechLocal Grant Application Support Material

### 1. Executive Summary
The **Global Intelligence Dashboard** is a real-time situational awareness tool designed to help UK businesses navigate an increasingly volatile global landscape. By aggregating live data on supply chains (shipping/flight paths), geopolitical risks (conflict zones), and market stability (news/indices), this tool empowers SMEs to make data-driven decisions, reducing risk and protecting revenue.

### 2. Business Case for Funding
**Problem:** UK SMEs utilizing international supply chains lack affordable, centralized tools to monitor global disruptions. "Enterprise-grade" solutions like Bloomberg Terminals or Palantir are cost-prohibitive (£20k+/year).
**Solution:** A low-cost, web-based dashboard aggregating free-tier public data (OpenSky, USGS, OSINT) into a single "pane of glass."
**Economic Impact:**
*   **Risk Mitigation:** Early warning of shipping route blockages (e.g., Rotterdam/Suez) allows businesses to reroute stock.
*   **Market Agility:** Real-time news feeds enable faster hedging against currency/commodity fluctuations.
*   **Accessibility:** Democratizes "institutional-grade" intelligence for local businesses.

### 3. Key Features & Data Sources
*   **Live Supply Chain Eyes:**
    *   *Rotterdam/Hong Kong Port Cams:* Visual confirmation of port congestion.
    *   *OpenSky Network:* Real-time flight tracking for air freight monitoring.
    *   *Chokepoint Layers:* Visual indicators for Suez, Hormuz, and Malacca straits.
*   **Geopolitical Risk Indicators:**
    *   *Pentagon Pizza Index:* Novel volume-tracking widget for predicting crisis events.
    *   *Conflict Hotspots:* Simulated data layers for active zones (Ukraine/Middle East).
    *   *Nuclear Infrastructure:* Mapping of critical energy assets.
*   **Multi-Source News Aggregator:**
    *   Simultaneous access to 8+ global networks (Sky, Bloomberg, Al Jazeera, etc.) to bypass regional bias.

### 4. User Guide
1.  **Map Navigation:**
    *   Use the **Layer Control** (bottom-right) to toggle overlays: *Flights*, *Nuclear Sites*, *Chokepoints*.
    *   Click markers for detailed popup info (e.g., Flight Origin/Destination).
2.  **Visual Confirmation (Webcams):**
    *   Scroll the **Bottom Panel** to view live feeds from key logistics hubs (Rotterdam, HK) and capitals (Taipei, Warsaw).
    *   Click a thumbnail to expand it.
3.  **Intel Feed:**
    *   Monitor the **Left Sidebar** for "AI Alerts" (Cyber/Infra risks) and the "Pentagon Pizza Index" for crisis probability.
    *   Use the **Quick Links** bar to verify data with external OSINT sources (LiveUAMap, ISW).

### 5. Technical Roadmap (Grant Funding Usage)
*   **Phase 1 (Complete):** Prototype with live public feeds and simulated analytics.
*   **Phase 2 (Funded):** Integration of paid maritime APIs (MarineTraffic) and custom AI sentiment analysis on news feeds.
*   **Phase 3:** Mobile app development and API access for third-party logistics software.
