The Foundation

The Market Maker Cycle

Every trading day follows the same institutional pattern.

🌒

1. Accumulation

Asian session. Market makers build positions in a tight range. Price consolidates. This forms the Asian Box — the anchor for the entire day.

⚡

2. Manipulation

London open. A fake breakout of the Asian range designed to trigger retail stop losses and build liquidity. The stop hunt before the real move.

📈

3. Distribution

The real directional move. Market makers push price to their target — often the opposite end of the Asian range and beyond. This is where you trade.

Step 1

The Asian Range ■

The starting point of every trading day.

  • 🕒Drawn automatically from your configured Asian session hours (default: 00:00–06:00 server time)
  • 📏Shows the high, low, and midline of the consolidation range
  • 🎯A break below = potential Buy T1 setup. A break above = potential Sell T1 setup
  • 📊Range size compared to ADR tells you if the day has room left to move

Core Concept

The Level System L1 → L2 → L3

Tracks how far the cycle has progressed.

L1
Consolidation
First Asian box. Direction unknown. No entries yet — wait for the market to show its hand.
L2
Trend
Second clear box in the same direction. Trend is confirmed. This is where you trade with the direction.
L3+
PF Zone
Extended move. Peak formation likely. Trade reversals only — look for PFH/PFL confirmation.
Rooms are drawn as outline rectangles on the chart • Color: Blue = Bullish direction • Red = Bearish direction • Grey = No direction
Reversals

Peak Formations

The market maker has reached their target. Time to reverse.

PFH — Peak Formation High

Bullish cycle at L2+ followed by a clear down break. The high is in. Market reversing bearish.

Trade: Look for SELL entries after PFH confirmation

PFL — Peak Formation Low

Bearish cycle at L2+ followed by a clear up break. The low is in. Market reversing bullish.

Trade: Look for BUY entries after PFL confirmation
PF alerts fire via Alert() + Push Notification + on-chart toast • Detected on H1 session boxes
Entries

The Five Entry Types

All based on the M15 EMA 5/13 crossover.

Type Condition Aggression Best At
Type 1 M15 EMA cross after breaking the Asian range 🔥 High — stop hunt entry London KZ, L1-L2
Type 2 M15 EMA cross inside the Asian range (no break) ⚠ Medium — range entry London/NY KZ, L2
Type 3 M15 EMA cross near the 50 EMA (Water line) ✔ Conservative — pullback entry Any KZ, trending
ID50 M15 Type 2 + near 50 EMA. Pullback to the water without breaking the range ✔ Conservative L2 trend continuation
Safety M15 EMA cross near 50 EMA after the range was already broken. Re-entry on pullback 🛡 Safest — confirmed direction Post-manipulation

Risk

Risk Management

The indicator provides levels. You manage the risk.

Stop Loss Placement

  • 🔴Type 1: Below Asian range low (buy) / above high (sell) + buffer
  • 🟠Type 2: Below the signal candle's low/high + buffer
  • 🟢ID50/Safety: Below the pullback swing + buffer

Take Profit Targets

  • 🎯TP1: ADR projection from the daily low (buy) or high (sell)
  • 🚀TP2: 1.5x ADR extension — for runners
  • 📊ADR > 90%: Skip the trade — daily range exhausted
"If the ADR is already exceeded, the move is done.
Don't chase — wait for tomorrow's cycle."

Golden Rules

Rules to Trade By

  • 01Trade with the direction at L1-L2. Don't fight the trend until a peak formation confirms.
  • 02L3+ = reversal zone only. Wait for PFH/PFL confirmation before counter-trend entries.
  • 03Trade during Killzones. London (08:00-11:00) and New York (13:00-16:00) have the volume.
  • 04Check the ADR. If daily range exceeds 80-90%, the move is likely done for today.
  • 05Use HTF confluence. H4 and D1 should support your direction for highest probability.
  • 06Scanner finds, M15 confirms. Let the scanner flag pairs, then confirm on M15 chart.
  • 071-2% risk per trade. Always. The indicator gives you the data — discipline is on you.
