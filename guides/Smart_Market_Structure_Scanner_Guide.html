1. What Is the SMC Scanner?

From v2.81 onward, Smart Market Structure Pro includes a built-in multi-symbol scanner. It is not a separate product — it lives inside the main indicator and reads the same Smart Money Concepts logic you see on the chart.

What it does

  • Monitors up to 30 symbols (default: 12 majors + XAUUSD, XAGUSD, US500)
  • Scans 4 timeframes per symbol (default: M15, H1, H4, D1)
  • Shows a traffic-light cell for each pair/timeframe: green BUY, red SELL, grey WAIT
  • Displays a confluence score 0–10 in every cell
  • Highlights the BEST setup in the banner at the top
  • Click any cell to open that symbol and timeframe on a new chart
One brain, three views: The scanner grid, the dashboard confluence meter, and the AI Trade Idea panel all use the same verdict rules. A cell that says BUY 6 on GBPUSD H4 will still say BUY when you open that chart (not flip to WAIT unless market conditions genuinely change).

2. Quick Start (60 Seconds)

  1. Install SmartMarketStructure.ex5 (MT5) or SmartMarketStructure_MT4.ex4 (MT4) and drag it onto any chart.
  2. On the dashboard, click the Scanner button (or set InpScnDefaultVisible = true in inputs to show it on load).
  3. Wait a few seconds for history to preload for all symbols and timeframes.
  4. Look for green or red cells with score 5 or higher.
  5. Read the gold BEST banner for the strongest setup right now.
  6. Click that cell to open the chart and confirm with the AI Trade Idea panel.
First time? Press ? on the scanner header for the built-in Quick Guide. The grid minimises automatically while the popup is open so you can read it clearly.

3. Reading the Grid

Each cell is one symbol + one timeframe. The text tells you everything in plain English:

Cell showsMeaningWhat to do
BUY 7 Bullish structure + price in Discount + score ≥ 5 Click to open chart. Confirm OB/FVG and use AI Trade Idea for Entry/SL/TP.
SELL 6 Bearish structure + price in Premium + score ≥ 5 Same — open chart and verify before entry.
WAIT 4 Trend direction seen but zone or score not ready Watchlist only. Do not force the trade.
WAIT 2 Often: correct trend but wrong Premium/Discount side Wait for pullback (e.g. bullish trend but price still in Premium).

Legend explained

  • Green background = actionable BUY setup (score ≥ 5, in Discount)
  • Red background = actionable SELL setup (score ≥ 5, in Premium)
  • Grey background = WAIT — conditions not met yet
  • Number (0–10) = confluence score for that cell
  • Gold outline = current BEST pick (also shown in the banner)
  • 3b in the Why popup = last BOS/CHoCH was 3 bars ago (freshness hint)
Press the small ? next to any setup (via the Why popup) for a plain-English breakdown: trend, Premium/Discount zone, Order Block, FVG, session windows, and why the score is what it is.

6. Chart & Scanner Alignment

A common question: “The scanner showed BUY 6 from my H4 chart, but when I opened M15 it changed.” From v2.82, the scanner and chart are designed to agree:

  • Current-chart cell (the row/column matching the chart you are on) reads directly from the live engine — identical to the dashboard and AI Trade Idea.
  • Off-chart cells use the same trend rules (confirmed BOS/CHoCH), Premium/Discount gates, and confluence weights as the main indicator.
  • Clicking a cell opens that exact symbol + timeframe. The cell for that chart then switches to live-engine mode and should match what you saw before opening (unless price moved).
How to verify: Note a cell (e.g. GBPUSD H4 BUY 7) from another chart. Click it. The H4 cell on the new chart should still read BUY 7 and the dashboard should show BULLISH with a similar confluence score.

When WAIT is correct (not a bug)

  • Bullish trend but price in Premium (wait for Discount pullback)
  • Bearish trend but price in Discount (wait for Premium rally)
  • Right zone but score still building (< 5)
  • Off-hours with no killzone/Silver Bullet bonus

10. Multi-Chart Setup

Many traders run one master chart with the scanner visible and several slave charts for individual pairs.

  • Master chart: InpScnDefaultVisible = true, scanner on, full symbol list.
  • Slave charts: Click Hide on the scanner, or set Scan when hidden = false to save CPU.
  • Use Quick Switch buttons on the dashboard to jump pairs without the Navigator.
  • Optional: InpPairStripFollowScanner mirrors the first 8 scanner symbols on the dashboard strip.
The floating Show Scanner button appears when the grid is hidden — one click restores it on that chart only.

11. Scanner Alerts

When a cell score crosses your alert threshold (default 7), the scanner can notify you via:

  • MT5 popup alert
  • Sound
  • Push notification (if enabled and configured in MT5)

Alerts fire when score increases to the threshold, not on every refresh, to avoid spam. Changing pairs on the dashboard temporarily suppresses duplicate alerts for the same symbol.

12. FAQ & Troubleshooting

Q: All cells show WAIT 0 or grey empty cells

History may still be downloading. Wait 10–30 seconds after attach. If H1 stays empty, check Market Watch — symbols must be visible. Try pressing RFRSH.

Q: Scanner rows are blank / wrong symbols

Your broker likely uses a suffix (e.g. EURUSDm). Set Broker suffix to AUTO or your broker’s suffix in scanner inputs.

Q: Scanner says BUY but dashboard says WAIT

From v2.82 this should not happen on the same symbol and timeframe. If it does, remove and re-attach the indicator to load the latest build. Make sure chart timeframe matches the cell you are comparing.

Q: Cell changed after I clicked it

If score dropped slightly (e.g. BUY 6 → WAIT 4), price may have moved out of Discount/Premium or an Order Block was mitigated. Small score wobble (±1) is possible; direction flips on the same bar are not expected in v2.82+.

Q: Can I use the scanner without the chart drawings?

Yes. Hide individual layers (OB, FVG, etc.) via dashboard toggles. The scanner still analyses structure internally. For the cleanest view, use Standard layout on the main guide.

Q: Is there a standalone scanner indicator?

The recommended setup is the built-in scanner inside SMC Pro v2.82+ so chart, scanner, and AI Trade Idea stay aligned. A legacy standalone scanner file exists for development but the embedded version is what ships with the main product.

Q: Scanner covers my help popup

Enable Minimize for popup (default on). The grid collapses while Quick Guide or Why-this-score is open.
