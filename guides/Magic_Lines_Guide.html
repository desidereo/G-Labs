📊 Indicator Overview

What Magic Lines v4 does and how all the components work together

Magic Lines v4 is a comprehensive multi-tool indicator for MetaTrader 5 that combines trend detection, cycle analysis, key price levels, and a multi-pair scanner dashboard into a single overlay. It draws directly on the main chart window and uses a canvas-based dashboard panel for the scanner.

Heiken Ashi Candles

Smoothed candlesticks with 6 color states that filter noise and reveal the true trend. Colors range from dark red (strong bearish) through orange (transition) to bright blue (strong bullish).

VWAP Line

Volume Weighted Average Price displayed as a bold yellow line. Acts as a dynamic support/resistance. Session-based reset options available (Session, Week, Month, Quarter, Year).

EMA 62 Trend Line

Color-coded Moving Average that switches between blue (bullish) and red (bearish). Price touching this EMA triggers "ML/touch" signals in the scanner.

Triple ZigZag System

Three nested ZigZag levels for multi-timeframe structure analysis:
Large Medium Small

Magic Lines & Levels

Automatically drawn horizontal support/resistance lines at key structural levels (APH/APL). These are the "magic" in Magic Lines — they identify where price is likely to react.

Multi-Pair Scanner

Canvas-based dashboard showing up to 28+ pairs with cycle state, APH/APL levels, ADR data, touch signals, re-entry status, and clickable pair switching.

🔧 Installation & Setup

Getting Magic Lines running on your charts

  1. Copy the indicator file

    Place check_magic_lines_v4.mq5 into your MT5 data folder:
    MQL5\Indicators\ (or a subfolder of your choice).

  2. Compile in MetaEditor

    Open MetaEditor, find the file, and press F7 to compile. Ensure zero errors. If you see the compiled .ex5 file, you're good.

  3. Attach to chart

    In MT5, go to Insert → Indicators → Custom and select Magic Lines v4. Alternatively, drag it from the Navigator panel onto any chart.

  4. Allow DLL imports

    The canvas-based dashboard requires DLL imports. In the indicator properties, check "Allow DLL imports" under the Common tab.

🎯 Trade Entry Lines & Signals

How to read and act on the indicator's trade signals

Entry Signal Types

EMA 62 Touch PRIMARY

Price pulls back to the 62 EMA and touches it. The scanner shows "Touch" in the ML/touch column. This is the highest-probability entry when aligned with the cycle direction.

  • Bull cycle + price touches EMA from above → BUY
  • Bear cycle + price touches EMA from below → SELL

Re-Entry Signal CONTINUATION

After a pullback within an existing trend, when the Heiken Ashi candles resume the trend direction for 2+ candles, a Re-Entry is confirmed.

  • Scanner shows re-entry direction (▲/▼)
  • Orange line and "Re-Entry" label on chart
  • Best when ADR has room (not over-extended)

Magic Line Bounce STRUCTURAL

Price reaches a Magic Line (horizontal level at APH/APL) and shows rejection. These levels are drawn from the ZigZag-identified highs and lows.

  • Watch for HA color change at the level
  • Multiple ML levels clustered = stronger zone
  • Best with VWAP confluence

VWAP Cross MOMENTUM

Price crossing the VWAP line signals a shift in intraday bias. Alerts fire for both up and down crosses.

  • Cross up → bullish session bias shift
  • Cross down → bearish session bias shift
  • Most reliable at start of London/NY sessions

✨ Understanding Magic Lines

The horizontal levels that give this indicator its name

Magic Lines are automatically drawn horizontal price levels that mark APH (All-Time Peak High) and APL (All-Time Peak Low) structures identified by the ZigZag analysis. These are the levels where institutional order flow is concentrated.

How Magic Lines Are Calculated

  1. ZigZag identifies swing points

    The triple ZigZag system (Large, Medium, Small) locates swing highs and lows across multiple depth levels.

  2. APH/APL classification

    Each swing point is classified as an APH (Absolute Price High — a peak that hasn't been broken) or APL (Absolute Price Low — a trough that hasn't been broken).

  3. Lines drawn at key levels

    Horizontal trend lines are drawn at each confirmed APH/APL level. These extend to the right as potential future support/resistance. Each is labeled (ML 1, ML 2, etc.).

  4. Age tracking (Days)

    The scanner shows how many days ago each APH/APL was formed. Recent levels (1-3 days) are most reactive; older levels (10+ days) are more structural.

⚡ Performance Optimization

Fixing lag, reducing CPU usage, and making the indicator run faster

Current Performance Bottlenecks

CRITICAL

iCustom Multi-Pair Loading (3x per pair)

Each scanned pair creates three separate iCustom handles — one for the current timeframe plus two additional timeframes. With 10 pairs, that's 30 indicator instances running simultaneously, each computing Heiken Ashi, VWAP, EMA, and triple ZigZag calculations.

Fix: Reduce scanner pairs to 5-8 maximum. Disable the second additional timeframe if not needed. Consider using a lighter "scanner-only" version of the indicator that skips chart drawing.

HIGH

Canvas Redraw on Every Timer Tick

The canvas-based dashboard redraws the entire panel on every OnTimer() call, even when no data has changed. Canvas bitmap operations are expensive.

Fix: Add dirty-flag tracking — only redraw cells whose values actually changed. Cache the previous scanner data array and compare before redrawing.

MEDIUM

66 Indicator Buffers + 65 Plots

The indicator declares indicator_buffers 66 and indicator_plots 65. Each buffer consumes memory proportional to the chart's bar count. On a chart with 100K bars, that's significant.

Fix: Audit which buffers are actually needed for display vs. internal calculation. Internal-only buffers can use arrays instead of indicator buffers.

MEDIUM

Chart Object Accumulation

Magic Lines, labels, and trend lines are created as chart objects. Without cleanup, these accumulate over time and slow down chart rendering.

Fix: The OnDeinit() already cleans up objects with the indicator prefix. Ensure ObjectsDeleteAll() runs properly. Limit the maximum number of historical ML lines drawn.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and troubleshooting

Why does the scanner show "--" for some pairs?

The pair may not have enough history loaded, or the symbol name doesn't match your broker's naming convention (e.g., "EURUSD" vs "EURUSD.m" vs "EURUSDm"). Check your broker's exact symbol names in Market Watch.

Why is MT5 slow after loading Magic Lines?

The indicator loads 3 iCustom instances per scanner pair. With 10+ pairs, that's 30+ indicator copies. Reduce scanner pairs, increase chart timeframe, and limit max bars in MT5 settings.

Can I use this with other indicators?

Yes, but since Magic Lines already includes Heiken Ashi, VWAP, EMA, and ZigZag, adding duplicate indicators wastes resources. Consider removing standalone versions of those tools.

The scanner panel disappeared — how do I get it back?

It may be minimized or positioned off-screen. Remove the indicator and re-add it, which resets the panel position. Alternatively, delete the GlobalVariables with the indicator's name prefix via MT5 → Tools → Global Variables.

What's the best timeframe for Magic Lines?

M15 and H1 are the sweet spots for forex. M15 gives more frequent signals while H1 gives higher-quality setups. The scanner lets you monitor multiple TFs simultaneously regardless of your chart TF.

Why can't I edit the source code?

The .mq5 file is obfuscated — all variable and function names have been replaced with short cryptic identifiers (e.g., c_w_, m_k2, f_j__). This is intentional IP protection. You need the original un-obfuscated source from G-Labs to make modifications.

How do I add more pairs to the scanner?

The pairs list is configured via input parameters. Look for the symbol array inputs in the indicator settings. Add pairs using your broker's exact symbol names. Remember: each additional pair adds CPU load.
